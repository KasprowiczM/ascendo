### Known limitations

- F1b (dashboard Python on Homebrew `python@3.x`): the long-lived dashboard
  process may hold stale lazy imports after a keg cleanup. **Not implemented** —
  recommendation: eager imports at `run_phases` start, or defer `python@3.x`
  formula matching against `sys.executable` keg. Risk is low: the dashboard is
  not a long-running daemon in normal use and `checkhash` covers the shell-side
  exposure.
- `test_sudo_gate`: all 28 tests in `adapters/macos/tests/test_sudo_gate.py` pass cleanly (0 failed, verified across 3 consecutive runs; the earlier mention of 2 PPID failures was an obsolete note from early bring-up before PPID mock isolation was finalized). All contract tests are green (759 passed, 2 skipped, 0 failed).
